Support families during the new driver journey

Permit2Drive gives driving schools, advertisers, and local partners a practical way to connect with families who are actively focused on driving, safety, and getting ready for the road.

For driving schools and instructors

When families ask how to track supervised driving hours, Permit2Drive gives you a simple, free resource to share. Families download the app and manage their own records, so there is no setup or administration required from your school.

It helps students and parents stay organized between lessons while working toward their state’s supervised driving requirements. Families should still follow their state’s official guidance and submission requirements.

Ways to share Permit2Drive

  • Add it to a parent resource page
  • Include it in a driver-ed welcome packet
  • Share the link after lessons
  • Print or email a parent handout
  • Mention it when families ask about driving logs
